About the System

E-JoseLib Digital Library

E-JoseLib is an AI-assisted digital library system developed for Saint Joseph College of Rosario Batangas Inc. It replaces traditional manual processes with an integrated web platform that automates visitor logging, book cataloging, circulation management, and institutional reporting. Enhanced with machine learning recommendation, smart search, and a hybrid AI chatbot, it brings modern library services to every Josephian.

System Capabilities

What E-JoseLib Offers

Digital Catalog

Keyword-based search and availability tracking for all library resources, eliminating manual index cards.

Circulation Management

Automated borrowing, return tracking, due date management, and overdue notifications for all users.

Smart Recommendations

AI-powered content-based recommendation engine that suggests relevant materials based on borrowing history.

AI Chatbot Assistant

Hybrid chatbot an AI to answer library queries and guide users through available resources.

Visitor Attendance

Real-time digital visitor logging with time-in and time-out recording, replacing handwritten logbooks.

Analytics and Reports

Automated reporting on visitor statistics, borrowing trends, and high-demand materials for administrators.

Partner Resource

DOST STARBOOKS

Aside from E-JoseLib, students and researchers may also tap into STARBOOKS (Science and Technology Academic and Research-Based Openly Operated KioskS), a free digital library developed by the Department of Science and Technology (DOST) through its Science and Technology Information Institute (STII). It brings science, technology, and educational resources to students nationwide, even in areas with limited or no internet access.

What You Can Use It For
  • Research papers, journal articles, and reference materials in science and technology.
  • Learning and teaching materials for Grades 1 to 12 and general education college courses.
  • Livelihood, entrepreneurship, and financial literacy videos and modules.
  • Lessons, activities, and quizzes for exam preparation such as PISA and TIMSS.
  • Educational videos and reference volumes contributed by DOST and partner institutions.

Institutional History

Saint Joseph College of Rosario Batangas Inc.

Saint Joseph College of Rosario Batangas Inc. SJCRBI is a Catholic educational institution founded and operated by the Oblates of Saint Joseph (OSJ), a religious congregation of priests and brothers whose first missionaries arrived in the Philippines in 1915. The Oblates carry forward the vision of their founder, Saint Joseph Marello, in forming servant leaders grounded in faith and service.

Some six decades ago, a devoted Parish Priest of Rosario, Batangas—Rev. Fr. Enrico Tunesi, OSJ—took all the effort and willingness to build an institution now standing as the hallmark of Catholic Education in the town. He sought the assistance of his Superior, Rev. Fr. Lorenzo Bo, OSJ, to secure approval from the then Bishop of Lipa, Bishop Alejandro Olalia, D.D., who gave his blessing for the institution.

The first building, composed of four rooms, a laboratory, and a library, was constructed in January 1956. On March 17, 1956, then Director of Private Schools Mr. Pedro Magno gave consent for the full operation of the institution. It started with forty-eight freshman students under the guidance of Mr. Jovito Magtibay as its first Principal. Four years later, the school received government recognition for its full Secondary Education program.

Today, the institution continues to grow, offering Pre-Elementary through Tertiary education levels. Now formally known as Saint Joseph College of Rosario Batangas Inc., it remains a pillar of Josephite-Marellian education in the province, guided by the mission to prepare students as competent, faith-driven, and service-oriented citizens.

Source: Oblates of Saint Joseph – Saint Joseph Province Philippines (osjphil.org)

1956
Founding of the Institution

Rev. Fr. Enrico Tunesi, OSJ, establishes the school with 48 students and the first building comprising four rooms, a laboratory, and a library. Mr. Pedro Magno grants official recognition on March 17, 1956.

1960
Full Secondary Recognition

The Philippine government grants full recognition of the secondary education program, solidifying the school's standing as a complete secondary institution in Rosario, Batangas.

1989
Campus Expansion

An additional 2,238 sq. meter land area is purchased through the efforts of late Provincial Superior Rev. Fr. Sancho M. Sison, OSJ, accommodating the Pre-Elementary, Elementary, Home Economics Building, Laboratory, and Learning Resource Center.

1997–1999
Elementary and Pre-Elementary Programs

Pre-Elementary Department is established in S.Y. 1996-1997 (recognized April 29, 1998), followed by the Elementary Department in S.Y. 1997-1998 (recognized December 15, 1999), broadening access to Catholic education in the community.

Present
Saint Joseph College of Rosario Batangas Inc.

Now a tertiary-level institution, SJCRBI continues its mission of Josephite-Marellian education from pre-elementary through college, serving the academic and spiritual needs of the Rosario community.
